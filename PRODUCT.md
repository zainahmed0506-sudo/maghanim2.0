# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with Node.js, confirmed by the requested implementation.

## Users

Organisations and decision-makers in the UAE and wider MENA region evaluating trusted, Shariah-compliant payment infrastructure.

## Product Purpose

Maghanim presents a credible entry point for Shariah-compliant payments infrastructure for the Gulf. The first page should establish trust and make a future enquiry or onboarding path easy to add.

## Positioning

Secure, governed payments infrastructure built for the UAE and MENA, with Shariah compliance as a central commitment.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on the marketing site from desktop or mobile and need a clear, bilingual first impression before exploring future solutions, governance, or contact details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Only a hero page is in scope now. The primary action is a non-navigating placeholder until its destination is decided. Arabic implementation must be straightforward to add later, including right-to-left layout and Arabic copy.

## Brand Commitments

Use the supplied Maghanim wordmark and Arabic mark, plus the supplied gold coin. The supplied “green design” and “hero page style” images are binding visual references: deep emerald, restrained gold, heritage authority, and a refined Islamic geometric field.

## Evidence on Hand

Supplied project assets: `logo.jpeg`, `arabic full logo.jpeg`, and `coin design.jpeg`. No testimonials, quantitative proof, pricing, customers, or legal claims have been supplied and must not be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make the purpose understandable at a glance.
- Treat trust, governance, and regional relevance as first-class.
- Keep the experience composed and premium rather than noisy.
- Keep the initial architecture ready for English and Arabic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The hero must be keyboard usable, respect reduced-motion preferences, remain legible at common device sizes, and support future RTL Arabic content.
